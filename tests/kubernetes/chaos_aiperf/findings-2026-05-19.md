## Blockers / follow-ups

1. `wait_for_phase(..., timeout=180.0)` is tight after operator restart.
   Suggest 300 s or `k8s_settings.longrun_startup_timeout_s`.
   Shared-helper concern, not a port regression — legacy `chaos/` files
   use the same default and would hit the same flake under back-to-back
   operator restarts.
2. No `faults.inject` adapter bugs found. The chaos_aiperf ports look
   ready for routine CI inclusion under `-m k8s_slow`.

## Confidence

The unified-adapter mechanism behaves identically to the legacy
injectors for the 6 scenarios that exercised it cleanly. The single
failure was environmental and would have occurred on the legacy file
too under the same back-to-back-restart condition. No structural
follow-up is required for the unified-API ports themselves.
